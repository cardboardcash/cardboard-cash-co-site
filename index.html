About Us

Cardboard Cash Co. is built by collectors, for collectors. We understand the thrill of discovering the next gem and the satisfaction of finding a new home for your cards. Our mission is simple: provide fair, honest deals and connect enthusiasts with the cards they love.

Our founder is a lifelong sports card enthusiast who values transparency, integrity and community. When you work with us, you’re not dealing with a faceless reseller – you’re partnering with someone who shares your passion.

Safety matters: never share personal details like your phone number or address. We handle all communications through secure channels and arrange exchanges in public places for your peace of mind.
